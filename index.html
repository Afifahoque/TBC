Sourcing & Recruitment Advisory

Identifying and vetting talent across Asian markets through ethical sourcing channels. We conduct one-on-one worker consultations to assess true skill levels, manage employer expectations, and build transparent shortlists grounded in mutual trust.

Comprehensive pre-deployment preparation including technical skill alignment, language basics, and cross-cultural workplace orientation—ensuring candidates enter European work environments fully prepared and confident from day one.
